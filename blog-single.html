AgriEye – AI-Powered Crop Monitoring & Field Intelligence Platform

Modern precision agriculture requires seamless synergy between embedded field telemetry and intelligent backend analysis. AgriEye bridges real-time IoT node data with cutting-edge Gemini Multimodal AI to diagnose crop anomalies and provide actionable agronomic guidance directly to farmers.

Architecture & Key Innovations

Built with a modular React frontend and high-performance FastAPI backend, the platform processes leaf imagery and sensor telemetry simultaneously. Gemini multimodal vision models analyze disease severity while rule-based engines synthesize localized treatment recommendations with low latency.
